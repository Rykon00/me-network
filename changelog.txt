Version: 0.5.11
  Features:
    - Krastorio 2 (issue #593): the ME recipes take Krastorio 2's materials in place of the vanilla stand-ins of AE2's materials, with AE2's counts: quartz for certus quartz (a quartz and a copper cable for fluix), glass for glass and quartz glass (also where AE2 has glass that had no stand-in: the ME IO Port, the ME Quantum Bridge), automation cores for the annihilation and formation cores, inserter parts for pistons, imersite powder for sky stone dust (256k and 1024k components), rare metals for glowstone (64k component, blank pattern) and diamonds (Advanced Card), lithium-sulfur batteries for dense energy cells (wireless terminal and module, Singularity cells), AI cores in the ME Quantum Bridge, imersium plates and energy control units in the 4096k and 16384k components, a charged matter stabilizer in the Singularity cells and an energy control unit in the crafting accelerators 256x to 4096x. The table is in docs/AE2.md, "With Krastorio 2". The startup setting "Krastorio 2 recipes and technologies" turns it off.
    - Krastorio 2: the ME Molecular Assembler takes the assembling machine 2's crafting categories as they are after Krastorio 2's changes, so it crafts Krastorio 2's assembler recipes too.
    - Krastorio 2 (issue #594): the ME technologies follow Krastorio 2's science tiers: ME 1024k Storage and its tier on utility science, 4096k with the matter tech card, 16384k and the Crafting Accelerators 2 with the advanced tech card, the Singularity cells with the singularity tech card (with Space Age, which Krastorio 2 does not support and where its singularity tech card cannot be researched, on the advanced tier), each after the Krastorio 2 technology whose items its recipes take (silicon processing for ME Network, rare metal processing for 64k, lithium-sulfur battery for ME Wireless, quarry minerals extraction, AI core, quantum computer, imersium processing, energy control unit, matter processing). Counts and times are those of Krastorio 2's technologies of the same tier (docs/AE2.md, "Technologies with Krastorio 2").
    - Krastorio 2 Spaced Out (issue #596): the same recipes, and the technologies on its order of the upper tiers: 4096k on production and utility science, 16384k and the Crafting Accelerators 2 with the advanced tech card, the Singularity cells with Spaced Out's space, metallurgic, agricultural, electromagnetic, matter, advanced and singularity cards, the ME Quantum Bridge after AI core (its quantum computer needs Aquilo).
    - Krastorio 2 (issue #598), groundwork: with Krastorio 2 or Krastorio 2 Spaced Out ME Network now loads after it and has a startup setting "Krastorio 2 recipes and technologies" (on by default) for the coming recipes and technologies on Krastorio 2's materials and tiers; nothing changes yet. With Gregtorio Continued its recipes stay.
    - ME Molecular Assembler carries the ME network (issue #560, as in AE2): pattern providers and assemblers that touch each other in any arrangement are one network, without cables in between; removing an assembler splits it like a cable. It has no power connection any more: the ME Controller draws its power, the full use while it crafts and a thirtieth of it while it is idle (shown in the controller's power list). While its network does not work it is switched off, with a status saying why. Your assemblers join the networks they touch with the update and need no pole any more; two networks that met at an assembler become one with two controllers (a conflict): you are told where, once, in the chat.
    - ME Logistic Storage Bus (issue #506): a block that makes a whole vanilla logistic network storage of the ME network. Place it inside a logistic network's area (the roboports' orange area) and connect it to the network: the terminal shows what the storage chests hold, autocrafting, export buses, interfaces and the terminal take from them, and the network stores into them after its drives (its default priority is -100). Three modes: "Storage" (the storage chests), "Storage and providers" (also takes from active and passive provider chests and buffer chests; the network still stores only into storage chests) and "Read only". Priority, filters, whitelist or blacklist, "filter on extract" and the upgrade cards work as on the ME Storage Bus, and are kept in blueprints, copy and paste and clones. One bus per logistic network; a second one says so. When the logistic network splits or its last roboport is removed, the bus follows within two seconds. Items that spoil, tools, ammunition, repair packs and items with tags or a label are not shown. Unlocked with the technology ME Network.
    - ME Terminal: filter the storage and crafting grid by quality (=, ≥, ≤) and sort by quality (issue #514). The filter sits next to the search field (only in a game with qualities): a comparator and "Any quality" or a quality. "Sort: quality" shows the higher qualities first, then by name and amount. The wireless terminal has the same filter.
    - ME Terminal: the quality filter also compares with <, > and ≠, like the game's filters (issue #520).
    - ME Logistic Interface (issue #531): a block that connects the ME network to the logistic robots. Place it inside a logistic network's area and connect it to the network; three hidden logistic chests stand on its tile. "Offer": rows of items (with quality) and amounts that the network keeps stocked for the robots, for requester chests, construction and players; requesters take from active providers and storage chests first, so the ME stock is the base's last source. "Request": rows "keep this many in the network": the robots bring what the network lacks, and the request drops to 0 at the target. "Accept": robots drop trash and surplus there ("Accept trash", on by default; a filter item makes it the preferred drop of that item). Everything that arrives goes into the network within a few seconds; what does not fit stays ("ME full"). Per logistic network an item is either offered or requested, never both: a request row of an item an interface of the same logistic network offers is red and asks for nothing (robots would carry it in a circle). 9 rows per tab, 9 more per Interface Capacity Card (up to 36); a Crafting Card crafts what an offer row lacks. Rows, switch, filter and cards are kept in blueprints, copy and paste and clones. Mined, the contents of its chests go into the network; destroyed, they are spilled. Unlocked with the technology ME Network.
    - ME Logistic Interface: "Request what crafting lacks" (issue #532, on by default, on the Request tab). When a level maintainer or a Crafting Card cannot start its crafting job because the network lacks raw ingredients, the interface asks the robots for them, the largest needs first, at most 20 items (a map setting); once they arrived, the next try starts the job. Per logistic network only the first interface asks; items offered in that logistic network are left out. What the starts lack is dropped when the job starts, the target is reached or nobody tries any more.
    - ME Logistic Interface: Automatic mode (issue #537, a switch at the top of its window; a newly built block starts in it, blocks built before stay in Manual). Without setting rows, the block offers what the requester chests, rocket silos, players and vehicles of its logistic network want and the logistic network cannot supply from its own chests, as far as the ME network has it: at most one stack per item (a map setting). Your storage chests are still used first. An item stays offered for two rounds after the demand is gone (robots on their way), then what no robot reserved goes back into the network. Items that ME blocks wait for (export buses, interfaces, level maintainers, crafting starts that lacked them) are not offered: the ME network comes first. The block also asks the robots for what ME blocks wait for, one stack per item, when the logistic network has it. With several blocks in one logistic network, the first one built reads the requests and the stock goes to the block nearest to the largest demand. Rows still apply on top; the window shows the mode, the counts and what is offered automatically.
    - ME Logistic Interface, Automatic mode: it also supplies construction in its logistic network (issue #538). Ghosts of buildings and tiles (a pasted blueprint), buildings marked with the upgrade planner and module requests are counted when they are made and dropped when they are built, cancelled or destroyed; what the logistic network cannot supply itself is offered like the requesters' demand (one stack per item by default, the ME network's own needs first), so construction robots fetch it from the block. The window says how many items construction needs there.
    - ME windows: your inventory on the left of every ME window is now the game's own inventory (Factorio 2.1, issue #542): one window, and in it the game's clicks, drag, hand, tooltips (a cell's or a pattern's description, spoil timers, labels) and trash keys. Shift + click puts a stack into the block or stores it in the network as before, control + click every stack of that item (now in every window: a drive takes every cell of the kind), shift + right click half the stack, control + right click half of every stack.
    - ME windows: the card slots of the ME Interface, Import and Export Bus, Storage Bus, Pattern Provider, Logistic Interface, IO Port and the boosters of the Wireless Access Point are the game's own slots too (issue #543), with the cards' tooltips; a card that does not belong there is still refused and stays in your hand. Shift + click takes a card into your inventory, control + click every card of that kind. The workbench, the IO port's cell slots and the drive keep their slots as before.
    - ME windows: a second number on slot buttons (Factorio 2.1, issue #544): the ME Terminal's storage and crafting tabs show above the stored amount how much the network's jobs are crafting of it; a job shows above its amount how much is done; a level maintainer row shows its stock and above it the amount to keep; the pattern slots of the pattern provider and the ME Interface Terminal show the amount the pattern makes.
    - ME windows play the game's machine window sounds when they open and close (issue #545), only for you; a window opened from another one (a cell from the drive, Back to the terminal) plays only the open sound.
    - ME Cable: drag it across an obstacle and the cables on both sides become a pair of ME Underground Cables, like belts (up to 10 tiles apart; the two underground cables come from your inventory and the two cables go back). Without underground cables, or for a wider obstacle, nothing changes. A shift drag makes ghosts of the pair. A gap with an ME block in it is left alone.
    - ME Underground Cable: hold the mouse button and drag to place the pairs at the full reach, exactly like underground pipes (the game's own drag; before, it put one on every tile). It now follows the vanilla underground pipe's rule: the cable side is the side it faces, the underground side the opposite one. The underground cables of your saves are turned around once with the update and stay connected; underground cables in blueprints made before keep their old direction and come out turned the wrong way: place them once and make the blueprint again.
    - ME Pattern Provider built or pasted from a blueprint (issue #558): the patterns it waits for are now filled from an encoded pattern of the same pattern in your inventory, then one in the ME network (taken out of it), then a blank pattern of your inventory, then a blank of the network; before, only the network's blank patterns were used. Robots building it use the network's patterns. While patterns wait, the provider shows "Waiting for N blank patterns" (yellow) on the map, its window has a line about it above the slots, and you are told once when you build or paste it (at the block, or in the chat with its place when robots built it).
    - ME Cable and ME Underground Cable replace each other in place, like pipes and underground pipes: place one on the other and the old one goes into your inventory.
    - ME Memory Card (issue #561, as in AE2): select an ME block with it to copy its settings onto the card (what a blueprint of it carries: rows, filters, cards, frequency, a pattern provider's patterns ...); alt + select an area to paste them onto every block of that kind in it. A pattern provider's patterns go into its empty slots, from encoded patterns in your inventory, then in the network, then encoded from blank patterns of your inventory, then of the network; what finds none waits. Select empty ground to empty the card; its label shows what it holds. Unlocked with the technology ME Network.
    - ME Pattern Provider: a settings paste (shift + right click, shift + left click) now also copies the patterns (issue #585): each pattern of the source goes into the same slot of the destination when that slot is empty, filled like a blueprint's pattern: an encoded pattern of the same pattern in your inventory, then one in the ME network, then encoded from a blank pattern of your inventory, then of the network; what finds none waits (yellow). Slots that hold a pattern keep it.
    - ME Pattern Terminal and Pattern Terminal Ex: "Clear patterns in inventory" turns every encoded pattern in your inventory into a blank pattern, and "Clear unused network patterns" every encoded pattern stored in the ME network that no pattern provider of the network holds or waits for (broken patterns always count as unused) into a blank pattern in the network (issue #585). Both ask first and name the count. Nothing is made or lost: when the network has no room for the blank patterns, the rest stay as they are and you are told how many.
    - ME Pattern Provider: "Return all patterns" in its window puts all its patterns into the ME network as encoded patterns and forgets the ones it waits for, so a provider is emptied in one click (issue #585); what the network has no room for stays.
    - ME Wireless Link: all links with the same frequency on a surface now form one wireless network (issue #559), not only two: a third, fourth ... link joins instead of linking nothing. A link joins when another link of the frequency is within its range (512 tiles), so links can form a chain. They are joined by the shortest connections that reach all of them, and each link's power grows with its longest connection. A link without power drops out and joins again with its power back. The window shows how many links share the frequency. Links of your saves that waited as a third link join with the update. The ME Quantum Bridge stays a pair.
    - ME Singularity Fluid Storage Cell (issue #562): one fluid, up to 536 870 912 units of it (2^29, the largest amount the network counts exactly to the game's smallest fraction of a unit). Works in the ME Drive, the ME Chest, the IO Port and the Cell Workbench like the other fluid cells. Recipe: the Singularity cell's ingredients and a pump; unlocked with ME Singularity Storage.

Version: 0.5.5
Date: 2026-10-23
  Features:
    - The Creative Energy Cell (issue #442, AE2's; no recipe: the map editor and cheat mode give it): an ME network with one never lacks power, also without a pole at its ME Controller. For testing and creative maps.
    - The Creative ME Storage Cell (issue #441, AE2's; no recipe: the map editor and cheat mode give it): partitioned in the ME Cell Workbench, it gives its network the listed items without end and destroys whatever of them is stored into it. For testing and creative maps.
    - View Cells (issue #440, AE2's; unlocked with ME Upgrade Cards, made from a storage housing and a stone): a list of items and fluids set in the ME Cell Workbench, with a Fuzzy or Inverter Card. The ME Terminal has five view cell slots in its storage tab: its list then shows only what the cells allow (what any whitelist cell names, never what a blacklist cell names). In the Wireless ME Terminal's window the slots are your own.
    - The ME Advanced Level Emitter (issue #443, AE2-Unofficial's; unlocked with ME Automation, made from an ME Level Emitter and a processing unit): six rows, each an item or fluid with its own threshold, active or not and inverted or not (on below the threshold), joined by OR or AND into one circuit signal. Its window shows what the network holds of each row. Blueprints, copy/paste and clones keep its rows.
    - The ME Pattern Provider can be locked by a circuit condition (issue #429, AE2-Unofficial's Lock Crafting Card modes, here without a card): it gets the small circuit connector of the buses, and "Lock crafting" in its window offers, next to "Until the result is back", "While the condition does not hold", "While the condition holds" and "Until the condition turns true" (after each hand-over, until the condition rises). These lock every hand-over of the provider, into its chests and its machines; without a wire or condition they lock nothing. Blueprints, copy/paste and clones keep the mode and the connector's condition.
    - The Crafting Card crafts fluids and has "Craft only" (issue #426, AE2-Unofficial's): a fluid filter of an ME Export Bus and a fluid row of an ME Interface that the network lacks are crafted like items. The products of a Crafting Card's job go straight into the export bus's target or into the interface (its inventory, or the tank of the side with that fluid), as AE2 hands them to the block that asked; what does not fit goes into the network. An ME Export Bus with the card can be set to "Craft only" in its window: it takes nothing from the network's storage and only crafts what its filters name while its target has room. Blueprints, copy/paste and clones keep the setting.
    - Crafting jobs merge (issue #418, AE2-Unofficial's): a player's request for an item that a player's job crafts already joins that job when its Crafting CPU has room for both; the Craft button says Merge, the CPU drop-down lists that CPU, and Automatic prefers it to a free one. Requests of level maintainers, Crafting Cards and the circuit interface never merge and need a free CPU as before.
    - The ME Storage Bus works on fluid wagons (issue #417): a fluid wagon in front of the bus is fluid storage of the network while it stands there, like a cargo wagon on the item side. The network sees its fluid at its temperature, fills it with that fluid and takes it out; when the train leaves, its fluid leaves the network, and the next wagon that stops there is read within a few seconds.
    - The ME Pattern Terminal Ex (issue #416, AE2-Unofficial's; unlocked with ME Autocrafting, made from an ME Pattern Terminal, an advanced circuit, a processing unit and an electronic circuit): encodes processing patterns of up to 32 inputs and 8 outputs, or 8 inputs and 32 outputs (a switch in its window), for recipes with more inputs or outputs than the ME Pattern Terminal's 9 and 6. Pattern providers, the planner and crafting jobs take such patterns like any other.
    - Crafting storage above 256k and the crafting accelerators (issue #415, AE2-Unofficial's): ME 1024k, 4096k, 16384k and Singularity Crafting Storage (a crafting unit and the storage component; the Singularity one 8 Singularity storage cells), and ME 4x, 16x, 64x, 256x, 1024x and 4096x Crafting Accelerators, which count as that many co-processors (each from four of the one below, the 4x from co-processing units). They join the rectangle CPUs like the other crafting blocks. New map setting "Crafting job operations per step" (1024): the machine hand-overs of one job step at most.
    - Storage cells above 256k (issue #414, AE2-Unofficial's): ME 1024k, 4096k and 16384k Storage Cells and Fluid Storage Cells with their storage components (63 item types, 18 fluids; up to 133 million items of one type in a 16384k cell), each tier from three components of the tier below, and the ME Singularity Storage Cell, one item type with practically no limit (up to 9 quadrillion items). Technologies ME 1024k to 16384k Storage and Fluid Storage and ME Singularity Storage, with utility science.
    - Circuit condition for the ME Import, Export and Storage Bus and the ME Interface (issue #412, AE2's Redstone Card without a card): each has a small ME Circuit Connector in the south-east corner of its tile. Attach wires there and set its condition in its window; while the condition does not hold, the block is off (a storage bus's storage leaves the network). The ME Export Bus has a pulse mode (one operation each time the condition turns true), the ME Interface can take its rows from the circuit (every item and fluid signal is a row of that amount). Blueprints, copy/paste and clones keep the wires, the condition and the settings; blocks of older saves get their connector on load.
    - The Crafting Card (issue #411, AE2's; unlocked with ME Upgrade Cards, made from a basic card and an assembling machine 1): in an ME Export Bus, a filtered item the network does not have is crafted (a job for what the bus would move, at most a stack); in an ME Interface (a fourth card slot), a row the network cannot fill is crafted (a job for what the row lacks). One job per item at a time; it needs a pattern and a free Crafting CPU, and a failed start is tried again a few seconds later. The window says what the card did last. Items only.
    - Capacity, Fuzzy and Inverter Cards on the ME Import Bus and ME Export Bus (issue #413, AE2-Unofficial's bus cards): up to 2 Capacity Cards give 9 more filters each (9, 18 or 27); a Fuzzy Card makes every filter take its item in every quality (the export bus exports each quality the network has, in turn); an Inverter Card, on the import bus only, makes its filters a blacklist: it takes everything else from its target, items and fluids. They share the bus's 4 card slots with the speed cards and the Group Filter Card. Filters beyond what the cards give room for are kept and work again when a card is put back; blueprints, settings paste and clones carry all of them and the cards. The recipe paste fills as many filters as the cards give.
  Optimizations:
    - Building many ME blocks at once is fast again in big networks (issue #420): a blueprint of 1000 blocks with storage buses took 1.9 s at 5000 and 7.3 s at 20 000 buses and interfaces, because every storage bus counted the controller's power over the whole network; now about 0.1 s. Removing many blocks at once takes about half as long at 20 000.
    - A mod update or a changed mod list keeps the ME networks as they were (issue #420): blocks that changed without an event are added or taken out, and the networks are rebuilt from the map only when that is not enough. A big base loads faster after an update (at 20 000 buses and interfaces 0.4 instead of 2.8 s), and the networks take about a quarter less memory.
    - The ME Interface has a hidden tank only on the sides that need one (issue #420): a side with a pipe, pump, tank or machine connected, a side tied to a fluid row, or a side whose tank still holds fluid. Pipes and machines connect as before: building, rotating or setting up a fluid entity next to an interface gives that side its tank at once, and anything built without telling the game is found within a few seconds. Older saves lose their empty unconnected tanks, never fluid. Click a card or cell slot with an item in hand to put it in; what does not belong there is refused with a message and stays in your hand. While a cell lies in the workbench its cards are items in its card slots; taking the cell out puts them into it. Cards on storage buses and cells in workbenches of older saves are kept.
    - The storage bus modes also show AE2's names (bi-directional, extract only, insert only). A blacklist storage bus is not preferred for an item over other storage of its priority (AE2's rule); a recipe pasted onto a storage bus changes only its filters.
    - The ME Crafting CPU, ME Co-Processing Crafting CPU and ME Quantum Crafting CPU can no longer be crafted: they are legacy blocks that keep working (several jobs of any size), and jobs running on them go on through the update on the same CPU. Their technologies unlock the crafting blocks (ME Co-Processing: the co-processing unit, 16k and 64k crafting storage; ME Quantum Crafting: 256k crafting storage).
    - A crafting job (the Craft button or a level maintainer) starts only on a free Crafting CPU that is big enough; it is no longer queued behind busy CPUs. A job whose CPU loses a block pauses with everything it holds and goes on on the next free CPU that is big enough (the rest of its own CPU too).
    - A level maintainer starts its job as soon as its item is taken below its amount (it was checked in turn: up to 40 seconds in a big network), and checks a circuit target at least every 5 seconds.
    - A storage bus sees a change made by an inserter or a player within 2 seconds at most (setting), never later than before.
    - More than 8 crafting jobs at once no longer slow each other down.
    - The circuit interface lists its signals by name (still the 1000 largest amounts) and updates at most once a second.
  Bugfixes:
    - A click on an ME block with a tool in hand opened the block's window and ended the tool: blueprints and blueprint books (also from the blueprint library), deconstruction and upgrade planners, the copy-paste and cut tool, selection tools of other mods, red, green and copper wires (a wire now connects to an ME Circuit Interface or Level Maintainer), ghosts, rails, spidertron and artillery remotes, capsules and items being built. The window now opens only when the game would open a chest's window: with an empty hand or a plain item.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-02
  Features:
    - The ME Interface handles fluids: its 9 config rows take an item or a fluid, and pipes connect to its four sides. Each side imports what is piped in (the default), is off, or keeps a fluid row's fluid at its amount (up to four fluids at once).
    - The ME Import Bus and ME Export Bus handle items and fluids: up to 9 filters, items and fluids mixed. On a machine with an inventory and fluid boxes (an assembling machine with a fluid recipe, a chemical plant) one bus does both.
    - The ME Storage Bus also works on a storage tank: the fluid of the tank and of every pipe and tank connected to it becomes network storage (as the ME Fluid Storage Bus did). Its 18 filters take items and fluids.
  Changes:
    - The ME Fluid Interface, ME Fluid Import Bus, ME Fluid Export Bus and ME Fluid Storage Bus are gone: no recipe, no technology unlock. ME Fluid Storage unlocks the fluid storage cells only; the unified blocks move fluids from the start.
    - Saves: every placed fluid interface, fluid bus and fluid storage bus becomes the unified block in place, with its settings and its fluid. Their ghosts, items (in inventories, chests, cells and blueprints), patterns and level maintainers get the unified block; old blueprints build the unified blocks.
    - ME Interfaces of older saves get their fluid sides when the game is loaded; a side that an existing pipe already touches is off, so a pipeline running past an interface is not drained (switch it to import in the interface's window).
  Optimizations:
    - Checking whether the network can store an item or fluid no longer copies the mod's prototype data for every storage cell; in the test network (two cells, seven storage buses) one check went from 0.10 ms to 0.025 ms.
  Graphics:
    - The ME Interface shows a pipe connection on each of its four sides.
  Bugfixes:
    - The ME Storage Bus, ME Import Bus and ME Export Bus did not work with the editor's infinity chest ("Faces no chest, logistic chest or cargo wagon").
    - The status line of the ME Terminal showed the fluid cell numbers shifted by one and "__13__" instead of the number of fluid cells.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-10-02
  Info:
    - First version as a mod of its own. The ME network was made in Gregtorio Continued (up to its 0.4.x) and moved out of it; Gregtorio Continued 0.5.0 depends on this mod and puts it on its GregTech tiers.
    - Saves of Gregtorio Continued with an ME network are taken over when they are loaded with Gregtorio Continued 0.5.0 and this mod: cells with their items and fluids, patterns, running jobs, level maintainers and the settings of every block.
  Features:
    - ME cables and underground cables connect ME blocks; a network runs with one powered ME Controller.
    - ME Drive with 10 slots for storage cells (1k to 256k, items or fluids) that keep their contents when taken out; cell partitions and drive priorities.
    - ME Terminal as the hub: storage with search and sort, crafting with a plan preview, jobs, the drives and cells, encoding patterns.
    - ME Interface, ME Import Bus, ME Export Bus and their fluid versions; ME Storage Bus on a chest, logistic chest or cargo wagon and ME Fluid Storage Bus on a tank's fluid segment.
    - Autocrafting: blank patterns encoded into crafting or processing patterns, ME Pattern Provider with 9 pattern slots, ME Molecular Assembler, ME Crafting CPU with two bigger tiers.
    - ME Level Maintainer (keeps an item or fluid in stock) and ME Circuit Interface (network contents on a circuit wire).
    - Works on its own with vanilla recipes and technologies (red and green science for the network, up to utility science for the biggest crafting CPU).
    - A data-stage API (ME_NETWORK, docs/API.md) for mods that want other recipes or technologies for the ME blocks.
