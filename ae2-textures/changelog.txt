Version: 0.2.0
  Graphics:
    - Storage cells (ME Network issue #247): the item icons of the 1k to 256k storage cells, the storage components 1k to 256k and the storage housing are AE2-Unofficial's. The fluid storage cells (ME Network issue #250), which AE2-Unofficial has no icon for, show its item cell of their tier with a blue frame and body.
    - Upgrade cards: the basic, advanced, capacity, overflow destruction, fuzzy, pattern capacity, sticky, inverter, equal distribution and acceleration cards have AE2-Unofficial's icons. The ME Interface Capacity Card (ME Network issues #251, #294), which AE2 does not have, shows AE2-Unofficial's Capacity Card with its capacity sign in orange, so it is not taken for the Pattern Capacity Card (dark sign, turquoise stripe), also without telling colours apart.
    - Patterns: the blank and the encoded pattern have AE2-Unofficial's icons.
    - Blocks (ME Network issue #260): the ME Drive, ME Chest, ME Interface, ME Molecular Assembler, ME Cell Workbench and ME Charger are AE2-Unofficial's blocks in the world, made from its block faces with depth to the east and south, and their icons are AE2 cubes. The working Molecular Assembler shows AE2's running lights. ME Network's drive and chest lights stay where they are (in AE2's bays and slot); the interface no longer marks its pipe sides.
    - The ME Pattern Provider (ME Network issue #269): AE2-Unofficial has no pattern provider block (in GT New Horizons the interface holds the patterns), so it is its purple interface skin, in the world and as a cube icon.
    - Cells in the ME Drive and the ME Chest (ME Network issue #264): a cell in a bay is drawn as AE2-Unofficial draws it, a piece of its cell texture (grey for item cells, blue for fluid cells) with ME Network's light as a single AE2 pixel in it, instead of ME Network's coloured bars. Needs ME Network 0.5.3 or later (its drive view); with an older one the bars stay. The cells and lights are hidden behind a character standing in front of the block like the block's picture (ME Network issue #267).
    - The ME Controller (ME Network issue #278): four AE2-Unofficial controllers side by side, as a 2 x 2 cluster of controllers looks in AE2, and an AE2 cube as its icon. Dark without power, AE2's running lights while its network works, AE2's red pattern when a second controller makes a conflict. The lights need ME Network 0.5.3 or later (its controller view); with an older one the controller stays dark.
    - The ME Terminal and the ME Pattern Terminal (ME Network issue #281): AE2-Unofficial's terminal panels, smaller than a block as in AE2, at the pixel size of the other blocks. Dark while their network does not work, AE2's screen in its Fluix colours while it does; their icons are the lit panels.
    - The ME Import, Export and Storage Bus (ME Network issue #282): AE2-Unofficial's bus fronts as blocks, and AE2 cubes as their icons. ME Network's plate on the side a bus faces and its arrows are drawn on the AE2 front, so the direction stays visible (AE2's front shows none). Needs ME Network 0.5.3 or later (its bus view); with an older one the buses look as without this mod.
    - The cable (ME Network issue #283): AE2-Unofficial's Fluix glass cable, 4 AE2 pixels thick, its pattern running on from tile to tile; the fluix cable item shows a crossing of it. The ME Underground Cable has no AE2 counterpart and stays ME Network's.
    - The blocks of a Crafting CPU (ME Network issue #302): the crafting unit, the co-processing unit, the 1k to 256k crafting storage and the crafting monitor are AE2-Unofficial's blocks, each a cube of its own as in AE2 (not ME Network's one slab): its plain block while it is no CPU, AE2's formed look (the coloured cross, the monitor's screen) while it is part of one. Their icons are AE2 cubes.
